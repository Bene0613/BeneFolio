Fictional Weather App

A creative weather experience for imaginary worlds

Concept & Idea

For this project, I created a weather app focused on fictional cities from movies, series, and cartoons such as Hogwarts, Gotham, and Bikini Bottom. Instead of using real-world locations, I wanted to build something playful and original that reflects my personal interests while still showcasing my technical skills.

Brainstorming

During the brainstorming phase, I explored several ideas including a traditional weather app and a satirical parody app. Eventually, I chose fictional cities because they allowed me to be more creative and design weather conditions that match the atmosphere of each world.

Development Process

The app was built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I structured the page with a city selector and a weather card, styled it using flexbox and semi-transparent layers, and added a CSS animation to create a looping, moving background. JavaScript handles the interaction by updating weather data, background images, fun facts, and optional background music based on the selected city.

Final Result

The final result is an interactive and visually engaging weather app featuring five fictional cities. Each city has its own animated background, unique weather conditions, and playful details that make the experience feel alive while remaining clear and user-friendly.
